Information

8 Bit Weather is a simple, effective and cute way to check the weather. This application is a lightweight weather app which captures and translates weather data from OpenWeather into hand drawn animations. The application displays current weather conditions, daily high and low temperatures, and brings each forecast to life through charming animated scenes. Designed to be simple and peaceful, 8 Bit Weather provides essential weather information in a fun retro style without all the fluff.

Current seasonal slide:

15 minute intervals

Weather data updates for this app makes a request to the data provider (OpenWeather) every 15 minutes. This app updates the weather every 15 minutes and immediately on boot.
